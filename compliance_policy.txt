[SENTINELPAY INTERNAL DEMO POLICY]
This policy file is an illustrative rule set created for the SentinelPay fraud-detection demonstration project.

It is not official banking guidance, legal advice, RBI regulation, AML regulation, or a real-world compliance standard.


[SECTION-101: HIGH-VELOCITY ANOMALY RULE]

If an account records more than 5 transactions within a rolling 24-hour period, the transaction is considered a high-velocity risk signal.

Recommended Review:
Send the transaction for additional manual risk review and investigate the transaction pattern according to the organization's internal procedures.

Risk Context:
High transaction velocity can be used as one signal when investigating potentially unusual transaction behavior.


[SECTION-204: GEOGRAPHIC LOCATION DEVIATION RULE]

If a transaction occurs more than 100 kilometers from the configured account baseline location, the event is considered a geographic-deviation risk signal.

If the transaction also occurs between 01:00 and 05:00 local time, the event may receive additional risk attention.

Recommended Review:
Evaluate the transaction together with other available risk signals before making an authorization or customer-verification decision.


[SECTION-309: UNUSUAL TICKET SIZE RULE]

Payments exceeding $500.00 may be considered an elevated transaction-size signal, particularly when limited account history is available.

If the transaction is also associated with an elevated anomaly score, the event should receive additional manual review.

Recommended Review:
Request appropriate verification and follow the organization's configured transaction-review procedures.

Note:
This demonstration does not automatically submit Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs), regulatory filings, or other legal reports.


[SECTION-412: BASELINE CLEARANCE RULE]

Transactions under $100.00 occurring between 08:00 and 20:00 local time, with transaction velocity below 3 transactions per day and location within 20 kilometers of the configured baseline, may be classified as lower-risk baseline activity by this demonstration system.

Recommended Review:
Retain the transaction in the audit ledger and continue monitoring according to the configured risk rules.
